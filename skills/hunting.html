Hunting

Overview

One of the misc gathering skills, Hunting revolves around tracking down and slaying beasts (and the occasional exception) out in the world for their resources.

The materials gathered vary depending on the target, and include items such as Scorpion Sting, G'loritch Skulls, and Pelts.

These materials can be used to craft various hunting-related and consumable items.

Hunters Machete.

How do I start to hunt?

In order to begin grinding the Hunting skill or gathering its resources, you are first required to craft and equip a hunting item, such as the Hunter's Knife.

You can craft these at any Smelter NPC.

Once you have crafted and equipped your hunting item, simply go and find the NPC you wish to hunt. Generally speaking, most hunted NPCs are classified as Beasts, however there are still exceptions to the rule.

Tips

Hunter's Machete. Offers significantly more experience, however a lower percentage to drop items, so be aware if you are wanting items over skill levels.

Both hunting weapons are usable across all classes, so any character can pick up the Hunting skill without needing a dedicated build.
