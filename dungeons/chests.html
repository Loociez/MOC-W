Treasure Chests

Chest Drops by Dungeon

Every treasure chest has a small chance (1 in 250) to also award a matching pet when opened, in addition to its listed rewards. Every chest additionally rolls a universal "bonus item" (crafting materials like Worm, Timber, Crappie, Rock, or Ore, with an occasional rarer Beginner's tool) — see the note at the bottom of this page.

Universal Bonus Items

Every chest opened rolls these on top of its listed rewards:

  • Bonus Slot 1 (always given): Worm, Timber, Crappie, Rock, or Ore
  • Bonus Slot 2 (occasional): Beginner's Rod, Beginner's Skill Kit, or Beginner's Axe
  • Bonus Slot 3 (occasional): Timber, Crappie, or Rock

Every chest also carries a 1-in-250 chance (guaranteed for the player "Rich") to award its matching pet, announced server-wide when it happens.
